# ADR 0086 — Field-aware invert of a prior ripple-close command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.79 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips (clip-id, rightmost), RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject, overwrite, and insert modes, and includeLinks=false), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, SetTrackState locked/unlock, overwrite / insert PasteClips, SetDissolve, RemoveDissolve, SetOpacityKeyframe, RemoveOpacityKeyframe, SetClipOpacity, SetClipTransform, SetClipCrop, SetClipColorBasic, and SetMasterAudio invert semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package. The OS clipboard stays closed. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats in this checkout, mid-clip insert paste invert, ripple-trim-lengthen invert, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips, leave-gap DeleteClip, or LiftClips still refuses `IMPLICIT_GAP`; that code and media-clip insertion order are not changed. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite that would overlap a followed cue and a dest remnant remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`. A topology edit of a dissolve host remains `TRANSITION_RANGE_EDIT_REQUIRES_REMOVAL`. A later same-time keyframe after removal still refuses `DUPLICATE_KEYFRAME` and is not retargeted. Invert still mints a new sequence version when clock-stripped `transitions`, `effects`, or `audioBuses` change, and still mints new clip and sequence versions when a clip's referenced effects are already in the bump set. Marker sidecar implementation is not in this checkout. Production edit algebra is unchanged in this increment.

## Context

Increment 1.78 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip `SetMasterAudio`. Range ripple-close — `RippleDeleteClips([], range: TimeRange(...))`, workbench 「Ripple close range」 — was already the admitted explicit TimeRange close, including a vacated leave-gap or insert-move source hole, and is not a fourth MoveClips mode. That command had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present. Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later `SetMasterAudio`, `SetClipColorBasic`, leave-gap trim of a remaining remnant, or a later move as success. Official conflict is invert of the tip, or a later leave-gap move of the packed right remnant that is a real write-set conflict.

Field invert records id-keyed list members and, until this slice, appended a missing entity at the end of its list. Restoring a mid-span track gap after a close therefore appended the gap after later remnants, and `ProjectDocument.fromJson` refused `IMPLICIT_GAP` even when the restored starts were the legal leave-gap layout. Production edit algebra already rebuilds internal gaps after each operation; invert must not change that algebra, must not retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`, and must not change media-clip insertion order just to officialize invert of a non-trailing leave-gap delete, LiftClips, or mid-span insert paste.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed range ripple-close of the vacated leave-gap hole `[1, 2)` after a leave-gap `MoveClip` of the right remnant by +1s, on a timeline split at 1s with hello (unmoved, clipLocal on the left remnant) and stay (followed, clipLocal on the right remnant), that is not the undo tip. Only that close command's still-matching write set is restored: the hole is reopened, later content is moved back, the left remnant and unmoved hello stay at 0, the right remnant returns to 2, stay follows back to 2.5, and sequence duration returns to 4. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the followed stay cue, or a later text edit of the unmoved hello cue, therefore does not conflict and is kept. Invert of the tip, or a later leave-gap move of the packed right remnant, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back.

Official workbench path splits at 1s, adds hello and stay cues, selects the right remnant, leave-gap moves it by 30 frames on the existing single-clip Move button, enters ripple-close start 1s and duration 1s, applies 「Ripple close range」, and a later marker. Extract is not required. Mid-clip insert paste invert, ripple-trim-lengthen invert, and auto-close as a MoveClips mode are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar. Persist-path checks after archive/fork use caption text and anchors, the later marker label, remnant start times 0 and 2, the duration of the remnant that starts at 2s, and sequence duration 4, not pre-fork clip or gap ids.

This slice keeps the Increment 1.73 and 1.78 invert version bumps. Persist-path invert after later markers already minted a new sequence version because clocksOff of the sequence object changes, and needed no further bump. `IMMUTABLE_VERSION_CONFLICT` is not retargeted.

The invert path in `packages/studio_document/lib/field_invert.dart` is extended in a bounded way: in-place field restores (including clip starts) run first, then a missing track gap (`items` member with `source == null`) is inserted by timeline start rather than appended. Media clips still append, so invert of a non-trailing leave-gap delete, LiftClips, or mid-span insert paste still refuses `IMPLICIT_GAP`. Production edit algebra, `RIPPLE_RANGE_AND_SELECTION`, and `RIPPLE_RANGE_NOT_CONTIGUOUS` are unchanged.

## Consequences

I-005 and I-010 stay partial. The field-aware invert matrix now has an official persist path for range ripple-close after later markers or later cue text edits, alongside the already-delivered invert paths for SetMasterAudio and the earlier visual, dissolve, paste, move, and topology commands. Mid-clip insert paste invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, ripple-trim-lengthen invert, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats in this checkout, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, the table's 0.02s off-grid offset, and I-006 remain out of this slice.
