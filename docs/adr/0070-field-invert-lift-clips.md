# ADR 0070 — Field-aware invert of a prior LiftClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.63 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips, LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, and SetTrackState locked/unlock semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package. Lifting a `clipLocal`-marker host remains `REF_NOT_FOUND`. An uncovering or cropping source-window interval remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`.

## Context

Increment 1.62 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip leave-gap `MoveClip`. `LiftClips` (the workbench Cut button) was already the admitted caption-aware leave-gap lift: later content is not shifted, a `clipLocal` cue bound to a lifted host becomes an absolute remnant at the same times, and captions do not enter the internal clipboard package. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present. Unlike the workbench Delete button, Cut is leave-gap even without extracting persistent audio.

A later leave-gap out-trim of the remaining left remnant remints the gap filler written in the vacated range and therefore conflicts with invert of the lift, the same filler-remint bound already documented for leave-gap delete invert and leave-gap MoveClip invert. Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later left trim. Invert of a non-last LiftClips that must reinsert an earlier clip is not this slice's official success path; this slice does not reorder inserted items or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed `LiftClips` of a rightmost remnant that is not the undo tip. Only that command's lift write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of an uninvolved earlier cue, or a later text edit of the leftover absolute remnant of the lifted clip's cue, therefore does not conflict: invert restores the lifted remnant, rebinds that leftover cue to `clipLocal` on the restored host, and keeps the later markers and revised text. A later leave-gap out-trim of the remaining left remnant remints the gap filler and is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT`, as is invert of the tip; neither rolls the database back. Invert of a non-last LiftClips that must reinsert an earlier clip is not this slice's official success path. Official workbench path uses multi-select plus Cut and a later marker; extract is not required. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar. This slice does not claim that invert clears the internal clipboard package.

## Consequences

A complete invert matrix remains unfinished. Auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, I-006, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, and syncLock remain unfinished. The table’s 0.02s AV offset remains off the 30 fps grid and still refuses `OFF_FRAME_GRID`; alignment stays at 1/30 s. Already-delivered ripple delete, ripple trim, leave-gap trim, leave-gap delete, move, lift/cut, split, extract, unlink/link, SetAudioTrackState, SetTrackState locked, invert of SetCaptions / SetMarkers / GroupClips / SetTrackState / SetAudioTrackState / UnlinkAudioVideo / ExtractAudioClip / SplitClip / leave-gap TrimClip / RippleTrimClip / rightmost RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / leave-gap MoveClip, overwrite, caption default/follow/interval/source-window, 1000 seeded random undo, and the opened section-3 geometry matrix stay unchanged. No new persistence table. No production algebra change.
