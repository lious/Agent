# ADR 0089 — Official persist path for caption-aware RollCut

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.82 as a bounded slice of the original I-006 remaining work (s3 roll row). I-005 and I-006 remain partial. This increment does not claim I-006 complete (slide and 1000 random legal I-006 inputs remain) and does not officialize field-aware invert of Roll or Slip, SlideClip, a complete invert matrix, mid-span insert-paste invert, or invert of a non-trailing leave-gap delete / LiftClips / ripple-delete. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. The command remains `RollCut(leftId, rightId, delta)`; this slice does not add `includeLinks`. Already-delivered invert semantics through Increment 1.80 and the Increment 1.81 caption-aware SlipClip path stay unchanged. The Increment 1.73 and 1.78 invert version bumps and the Increment 1.79 invert-path gap insertion stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and must not silently become Roll or Slip. The Increment 1.81 Slip button stays `SlipClip`. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package. The OS clipboard stays closed. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar. Marker sidecar implementation is not in this checkout. This slice does not open SlideClip, auto-close as a MoveClips mode, new-track, cross-project bake, source-interval crop, or pointer-drag Roll as a new gesture mode. Current algebra already refuses `RollCut` when any transition exists; that `TRANSITION_RANGE_EDIT_REQUIRES_REMOVAL` refusal is kept, and the official persist path is free of dissolves. The s3 note that transitions move with the cut is not this slice's official success path. Uncovering or cropping a source-anchored cue remains whole-group `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE` and is never silently converted to absolute or cropped. `NOT_ADJACENT`, `SOURCE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `CLIP_TOO_SHORT`, and `FIELD_LOCKED` stay whole-group and are not retargeted. Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. Production edit algebra of already-delivered commands, insertion order, and `IMPLICIT_GAP` are unchanged.

## Context

Domain algebra for `RollCut` and some no-caption tests already existed. On a continuous adjacent cut, left timeline duration and source duration grow by δ (source duration scaled by the left rate); right start, duration, sourceStart, and sourceDuration shrink by δ (source times scaled by the right rate); both outer ends and sequence duration stay fixed; both resulting durations must stay > 0 and handles/source windows must suffice. Until this increment, `applyEdit` and persistent-audio dispatch refused `RollCut` with `CAPTION_SYNC_EDIT_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` whenever the project had captions (the allowlist included SlipClip after Increment 1.81, but not RollCut). Persistent-audio dispatch also refused `RollCut` without captions as `AUDIO_TIMELINE_EDIT_MODE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. The workbench had no official Roll control.

After a 1s split of a 3s clip, the packed cut is at 1s. A legal positive roll of +15 frames (`Q.of(1, 2)` on the 30fps grid) moves the cut to 1.5s: left duration and source duration become 1.5, right starts at 1.5 with duration 1.5 and source start 1.5, sequence duration stays 3. A third remnant, if present, stays put.

## Decision

Add `RollCut` to the caption allowlists in `edit_algebra.dart` and `persistent_audio.dart`. After a roll apply, call the existing helpers consistently: `applySourceCaptionAbsolutes` so source-anchored cues reproject while the new source window still covers them, then `followClipLocalCaptions` so clipLocal cues on the right follow the moved right start. ClipLocal cues on the left stay put because the left start is unchanged. Absolute cues stay at author time. Do not invent a new caption rewriter. Do not invent a new command or change the command shape.

On the persistent-audio path, also admit linked roll at the same cut: each link-group member is paired with its same-track adjacent partner and rolled using that member's own source rate. Official persist path does not require extract.

Official persist path: import/split at 1s, add hello (`clipLocal` on the left remnant) and stay (`clipLocal` on the right remnant), then `RollCut` of the packed left/right pair by +15 frames. Hello stays at 0; stay follows the right start from 1.5 to 2; sequence duration stays 3. Then linear undo/redo, archive/fork (assert by remnant times, source starts, and cue text, not pre-fork ids), and export a VTT sidecar. Preview and MP4 stay `CAPTION_RENDER_POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. Official later work may include an absolute marker at 2s that stays at author time.

Official conflict / refusal stays whole-group, with no partial members: a non-adjacent cut (`NOT_ADJACENT`), source head/tail shortfall (`SOURCE_OUT_OF_RANGE`), `FIELD_LOCKED`, and `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE` if a source-anchored cue would leave or straddle the new windows. Those codes are not retargeted. `CLIP_TOO_SHORT` is not retargeted; a zero-duration roll still uses the existing video-only `INVALID_RANGE` check.

The workbench adds an official Roll control consistent with existing trim/ripple/slip buttons: one persistent `RollCut` of the selected clip and its adjacent same-track neighbor (preferring the later packed neighbor so the official path can select the left remnant), using the existing `edit-frames` numeric path. Tests go through the real button. This slice does not invent pointer-drag Roll as a new gesture mode.

## Consequences

I-006 stays partial: caption-aware RollCut now has an official persist path, but slide, 1000 random legal I-006 inputs, field-aware invert of Roll or Slip, and the rest of the I-006 matrix remain unfinished. I-005 stays partial. Mid-clip insert paste invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats in this checkout, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, and the table's 0.02s off-grid offset remain out of this slice.

Production change is limited to the caption dispatch allowlists, the post-roll existing caption/marker helpers, persistent-audio linked RollCut (previously `AUDIO_TIMELINE_EDIT_MODE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`), and the official Roll workbench button. The no-caption video-only roll algebra is unchanged. The Increment 1.81 slip caption path is unchanged. Invert production code is unchanged.
