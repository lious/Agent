# ADR 0076 — Field-aware invert of a prior insert-mode PasteClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.69 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject, overwrite, and insert modes, and includeLinks=false), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, SetTrackState locked/unlock, overwrite PasteClips, and insert paste semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package; invert of PasteClips does not claim to clear that package. The OS clipboard stays closed. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, mid-clip insert paste invert, SetDissolve invert, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite that would overlap a followed cue and a dest remnant remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`.

## Context

Increment 1.66 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip insert-mode `MoveClips`. Increment 1.68 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip overwrite-mode `PasteClips`. Insert-mode `PasteClips` was already the admitted caption-aware insert paste: it copies a payload, inserts at a cut, grows sequence duration by the package span, and shifts later remnants and followed cues without vacating or removing the source. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present. Insert paste at a cut is distinct from insert-mode move, which leaves a vacated source gap and does not auto-close it.

Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later mid-clip insert or SetDissolve. A later leave-gap `MoveClip` of the dest-shifted right remnant, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed insert-mode `PasteClips` of a copied left remnant at the 1s cut that is not the undo tip. Only that command's insert-paste write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the followed stay cue, or a later text edit of the unmoved hello cue, therefore does not conflict: invert removes the pasted clip, restores the dest-shifted right remnant's start and the sequence duration, restores the followed stay cue, keeps the unmoved left remnant and its followed hello cue, and keeps the later markers and revised text. Insert paste still grows duration by the package span and does not remove or vacate the source left remnant. A later leave-gap `MoveClip` of the dest-shifted right remnant, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back. Official workbench path splits at 1s, adds hello and stay cues, copies the left remnant, sets collision mode to insert, pastes at 1s — not the single-clip Move button — and a later marker; extract is not required. Mid-clip insert paste invert, SetDissolve invert, and auto-close are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar.

## Consequences

I-005 and I-010 stay partial. The field-aware invert matrix now has an official persist path for insert-mode `PasteClips` after later markers or later cue text edits, alongside the already-delivered invert paths through overwrite-mode PasteClips. Mid-clip insert paste invert, SetDissolve invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, the table's 0.02s off-grid offset, and I-006 remain out of this slice.
