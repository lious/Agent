# ADR 0072 — Field-aware invert of a prior overwrite-mode MoveClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.65 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject and overwrite modes), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, and SetTrackState locked/unlock semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, insert-mode MoveClips invert, `includeLinks=false` invert, overwrite PasteClips invert, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite that would overlap a followed cue and a dest remnant remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`.

## Context

Increment 1.64 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip reject-mode leave-gap `MoveClips` of both remnants of a split. Overwrite-mode `MoveClips` was already the admitted caption-aware overwrite move: the moving cue follows its host, a destination remnant is carved rather than shifted later, and a cue wholly inside the carved interval is kept as an absolute remnant rather than dropped. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present.

Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later out-trim of the carved destination remnant, because that trim changes a duration already recorded in the overwrite write set and is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT`. Native preview of a caption-bearing timeline still refuses `CAPTION_RENDER_POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; that refusal does not block committing the pending MoveClips gesture.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed overwrite-mode `MoveClips` of a left remnant onto a right remnant that is not the undo tip. Only that command's overwrite write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the leftover carved cue, or a later text edit of the followed moving cue, therefore does not conflict: invert restores the moving remnant's start, restores the carved destination's start and duration, rebinds the leftover carved cue to `clipLocal` on that host, restores the followed moving cue, and keeps the later markers and revised text. A later leave-gap out-trim of the carved destination remnant, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back. Official workbench path uses collision-mode overwrite, multi-select of the left remnant, preview, and commit — not the single-clip Move button — and a later marker; extract is not required. Insert mode, `includeLinks=false`, and overwrite paste are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar.

## Consequences

A complete invert matrix remains unfinished. Auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, I-006, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, and syncLock remain unfinished. The table’s 0.02s AV offset remains off the 30 fps grid and still refuses `OFF_FRAME_GRID`; alignment stays at 1/30 s. Already-delivered ripple delete, ripple trim, leave-gap trim, leave-gap delete, move (reject and overwrite), lift/cut, split, extract, unlink/link, SetAudioTrackState, SetTrackState locked, invert of SetCaptions / SetMarkers / GroupClips / SetTrackState / SetAudioTrackState / UnlinkAudioVideo / ExtractAudioClip / SplitClip / leave-gap TrimClip / RippleTrimClip / rightmost RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / leave-gap MoveClip / LiftClips / reject-mode MoveClips, overwrite, caption default/follow/interval/source-window, 1000 seeded random undo, and the opened section-3 geometry matrix stay unchanged. No new persistence table. No production algebra change.
