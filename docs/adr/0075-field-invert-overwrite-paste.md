# ADR 0075 — Field-aware invert of a prior overwrite-mode PasteClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.68 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject, overwrite, and insert modes, and includeLinks=false), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, SetTrackState locked/unlock, and overwrite / insert paste semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package; invert of PasteClips does not claim to clear that package. The OS clipboard stays closed. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve invert, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite that would overlap a followed cue and a dest remnant remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`.

## Context

Increment 1.65 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip overwrite-mode `MoveClips`. Overwrite-mode `PasteClips` was already the admitted caption-aware overwrite paste: it copies a payload, carves intersecting destination intervals, keeps dest cue text, and may rebind a uniquely covered leftover cue to the pasted clip. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present.

Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later leave-gap out-trim of the carved destination remnant. A first review found that such an out-trim can change unrecorded `sourceRange` canonicity so `fieldInvertConflicts` stays false while invert then refuses `TIME_NON_CANONICAL`. Official conflict is invert of the tip, or a later leave-gap move of the carved destination remnant that changes a recorded start. This slice does not retarget that error code.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed overwrite-mode `PasteClips` of a copied left remnant onto a right remnant at 1s that is not the undo tip. Only that command's overwrite-paste write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the leftover carved cue, or a later text edit of the unmoved stay cue, therefore does not conflict: invert removes the pasted clip, restores the carved destination's start and duration, rebinds the leftover carved cue to `clipLocal` on that host, keeps the unmoved left remnant and its followed hello cue, and keeps the later markers and revised text. A later leave-gap `MoveClip` of the carved destination remnant, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back. Official workbench path splits at 1s, adds hello, carved, and stay cues, copies the left remnant, sets collision mode to overwrite, pastes at 1s — not the single-clip Move button — and a later marker; extract is not required. Insert paste invert, SetDissolve invert, and auto-close are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar.

## Consequences

I-005 and I-010 stay partial. The field-aware invert matrix now has an official persist path for overwrite-mode `PasteClips` after later markers or later cue text edits, alongside the already-delivered invert paths through includeLinks=false MoveClips. Insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, the table's 0.02s off-grid offset, and I-006 remain out of this slice.
