# ADR 0073 — Field-aware invert of a prior insert-mode MoveClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.66 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject, overwrite, and insert modes), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, and SetTrackState locked/unlock semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, `includeLinks=false` invert, overwrite or insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite that would overlap a followed cue and a dest remnant remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`. Insert-mode MoveClips still leaves a vacated source gap and does not ripple-close it.

## Context

Increment 1.64 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip reject-mode leave-gap `MoveClips` of both remnants of a split. Increment 1.65 officially admitted invert of a prior non-tip overwrite-mode `MoveClips` of a left remnant onto a right remnant. Insert-mode `MoveClips` was already the admitted caption-aware insert move: it opens a package-span hole at the destination and shifts later content, but the source is vacated as a gap and is not ripple-closed. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions present.

Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later leave-gap move of the destination-shifted remnant, because that move changes a start already recorded in the insert write set and is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT`. Native preview of a caption-bearing timeline still refuses `CAPTION_RENDER_POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; that refusal does not block committing the pending MoveClips gesture.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed insert-mode `MoveClips` of a left remnant by +1s that is not the undo tip. Only that command's insert write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the followed destination-shifted cue, or a later text edit of the followed moving cue, therefore does not conflict: invert restores the moving remnant's start, restores the destination-shifted remnant's start, restores sequence duration 3, restores both followed cues, and keeps the later markers and revised text. The vacated source gap remains a gap after the insert and is not auto-closed; invert puts the moving remnant back into that hole. A later leave-gap `MoveClip` of the destination-shifted remnant, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back. Official workbench path uses collision-mode insert, multi-select of the left remnant, preview, and commit — not the single-clip Move button — and a later marker; extract is not required. `includeLinks=false` invert, overwrite or insert paste, and auto-close are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar.

## Consequences

I-005 and I-010 stay partial. The field-aware invert matrix now has an official persist path for insert-mode `MoveClips` of a left remnant after later markers or later followed-cue text edits, alongside the already-delivered invert paths for SetCaptions, SetMarkers, GroupClips / UngroupClips, SetTrackState, SetAudioTrackState, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, ExtractAudioClip, SplitClip, leave-gap TrimClip, RippleTrimClip, rightmost RippleDeleteClips, leave-gap DeleteClip, leave-gap MoveClip, LiftClips, reject-mode leave-gap MoveClips, and overwrite-mode MoveClips. `includeLinks=false` invert, overwrite or insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, the table's 0.02s off-grid offset, and I-006 remain out of this slice.
