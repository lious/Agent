# ADR 0090 — Official persist path for caption-aware SlideClip

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.83 as a bounded slice of the original I-006 remaining work (s3 slide row). I-005 and I-006 remain partial. This increment does not claim I-006 complete (1000 random legal I-006 inputs remain) and does not officialize field-aware invert of Slide, Slip, or Roll, a complete invert matrix, mid-span insert-paste invert, or invert of a non-trailing leave-gap delete / LiftClips / ripple-delete. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. The command remains `SlideClip(leftId, clipId, rightId, delta)`; this slice does not add `includeLinks` or rename fields. Already-delivered invert semantics through Increment 1.80, the Increment 1.81 caption-aware SlipClip path, the Increment 1.82 caption-aware RollCut path, C.1–C.3 caption production, and A.1 audio bus production stay unchanged. The Increment 1.73 and 1.78 invert version bumps and the Increment 1.79 invert-path gap insertion stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and must not silently become Roll, Slip, or Slide. The Increment 1.81 Slip button stays `SlipClip`. The Increment 1.82 Roll button stays `RollCut`. Captions still do not enter the internal clipboard package. The OS clipboard stays closed. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar. This slice does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, new-track, cross-project bake, source-interval crop, I-007, an underrun hunt, or xd10 work. Current algebra already refuses `SlideClip` when any transition exists; that `TRANSITION_RANGE_EDIT_REQUIRES_REMOVAL` refusal is kept, and the official persist path is free of dissolves. Uncovering or cropping a source-anchored cue remains whole-group `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE` and is never silently converted to absolute or cropped. `NOT_ADJACENT`, `SOURCE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, and `FIELD_LOCKED` stay whole-group and are not retargeted. Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. Production edit algebra of already-delivered commands, insertion order, and `IMPLICIT_GAP` are unchanged.

## Context

Domain algebra for `SlideClip` and a no-caption conservation test already existed. On a packed adjacent triple, left timeline duration and source duration grow by δ (source duration scaled by the left rate); the middle timeline start moves by δ while its duration and sourceRange stay; right start moves by δ and its duration and source window shrink by δ (source times scaled by the right rate); the left start and the triple's outer end stay put, so sequence duration of a packed 3s example stays 3. Zero delta is a no-op. Until this increment, `applyEdit` and persistent-audio dispatch refused `SlideClip` with `CAPTION_SYNC_EDIT_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` whenever the project had captions (the allowlist included SlipClip after Increment 1.81 and RollCut after Increment 1.82, but not SlideClip). Persistent-audio dispatch also refused `SlideClip` without captions as `AUDIO_TIMELINE_EDIT_MODE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. The workbench had no official Slide control.

After splits at 1s and 2s of a 3s clip, the packed triple is left 0–1, middle 1–2, right 2–3. A legal positive slide of the middle by +15 frames (`Q.of(1, 2)` on the 30fps grid) yields left duration and source duration 1.5, middle start 1.5 with duration 1 and sourceRange unchanged, right start 2.5 with duration 0.5 and source start 2.5, sequence duration 3.

## Decision

Add `SlideClip` to the caption allowlists in `edit_algebra.dart` and `persistent_audio.dart`. After a slide apply, call the existing helpers consistently, following the Roll-like path: `applySourceCaptionAbsolutes` so source-anchored cues reproject while the new source window still covers them, then `followClipLocalCaptions` so clipLocal cues on the middle and right follow the moved starts, then `applyClipLocalMarkerLocals`. ClipLocal cues on the left stay put because the left start is unchanged. Absolute cues stay at author time. Do not invent a new caption rewriter or follow mode. Do not invent a new command or change the command shape.

On the persistent-audio path, also admit linked slide of the same packed triple: each link-group member is paired with its same-track packed left and right partners and slid using that member's own source rates. Official persist path does not require extract.

Official persist path: import/split at 1s and 2s, add hello (`clipLocal` on the left remnant), a clipLocal cue on the middle, and stay (`clipLocal` on the right remnant), then `SlideClip` of the packed triple by +15 frames. Hello stays at 0; the middle cue follows from 1 to 1.5; stay follows the right start from 2 to 2.5; sequence duration stays 3. Then linear undo/redo, archive/fork (assert by remnant times, source starts, and cue text, not pre-fork ids), and export a VTT sidecar. Preview and MP4 stay `CAPTION_RENDER_POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.

Official conflict / refusal stays whole-group, with no partial members: a non-adjacent triple (`NOT_ADJACENT`), source head/tail shortfall (`SOURCE_OUT_OF_RANGE`), `FIELD_LOCKED`, and `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE` if a source-anchored cue would leave or straddle the new windows. Those codes are not retargeted.

The workbench adds an official Slide control consistent with existing trim/ripple/slip/roll buttons: one persistent `SlideClip` of a packed same-track triple around the selected clip (the selected clip as middle when both packed neighbors exist; otherwise a left or right selection expands to the packed triple when both remaining neighbors are packed), using the existing `edit-frames` numeric path. Tests go through the real button. This slice does not invent pointer-drag Slide as a new gesture mode.

## Consequences

I-006 stays partial: caption-aware SlideClip now has an official persist path, but 1000 random legal I-006 inputs, field-aware invert of Slide, Slip, or Roll, and the rest of the I-006 matrix remain unfinished. I-005 stays partial. Mid-clip insert paste invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, and the table's 0.02s off-grid offset remain out of this slice.

Production change is limited to the caption dispatch allowlists, the post-slide existing caption/marker helpers, persistent-audio linked SlideClip (previously `AUDIO_TIMELINE_EDIT_MODE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`), and the official Slide workbench button. The no-caption video-only slide algebra is unchanged. The Increment 1.81 slip and Increment 1.82 roll caption paths are unchanged. Invert production code is unchanged. C.1–C.3 caption production and A.1 audio bus production are unchanged.
