# ADR 0074 — Field-aware invert of a prior includeLinks=false MoveClips command

## Status

Accepted for Increment 1.67 as a bounded slice of the original I-005 remaining work on the field-aware invert matrix (s3 / s5a). I-005 and I-010 remain partial. This increment does not claim a complete invert matrix. Official InversePatch `kind` stays `restoreSnapshot`. Linear undo/redo stay tip snapshot restores. Already-delivered RippleDeleteClips, RippleTrimClip, leave-gap TrimClip, leave-gap DeleteClip, MoveClip / MoveClips (including reject, overwrite, and insert modes, and includeLinks=false), LiftClips/Cut, SplitClip, ExtractAudioClip, UnlinkAudioVideo / LinkAudioVideo, SetAudioTrackState, and SetTrackState locked/unlock semantics stay unchanged. The single-clip Move button stays `MoveClip` and does not silently take an unlinked audio partner or expand into a group move. Video and caption mute/solo stay refused at admission with `FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; audio muted/solo stay `SetAudioTrackState`; `syncLock` is not toggled. This increment does not open auto-close as a MoveClips mode, or open new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, overwrite or insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve invert, or I-006. A diverging unlink invert remains `LINK_OFFSET_MISMATCH`. Invert's version indexer continues to skip source refs (`streamId` present). Leaf-level rational `n`/`d` granularity is unchanged. This slice does not change field-invert list insertion order or retarget `IMPLICIT_GAP`. An overwrite that would uncover or crop a source cue remains `ANCHOR_OUT_OF_RANGE`. An overwrite, or a later ignore-links move, that would overlap a followed cue and an unmoved absolute cue remains `CAPTION_OVERLAP`. includeLinks=false still moves only the explicit IDs, marks a partially moved AV link `allowOutOfSync`, and does not rewrite stored offsets.

## Context

Increments 1.64–1.66 officially admitted invert of prior non-tip reject-mode, overwrite-mode, and insert-mode `MoveClips` while captions were present. `includeLinks=false` was already the admitted same-command exception: it moves only the explicit IDs, leaves an unselected AV partner in place, marks the link `allowOutOfSync`, and does not rewrite stored offsets. A clipLocal cue follows only the moved host. It had not been qualified as an invert-matrix target on a persist path with captions and a linked audio partner present.

Official later work is a marker or a text edit, not a later second +1s ignore-links move of the same host, because that later +1s move would slide the followed cue onto the unmoved absolute stay and refuse `CAPTION_OVERLAP`. Official conflict is invert of the tip, or a later +0.5s includeLinks=false move that still changes the recorded host start without overlapping the unmoved stay. Native preview of a caption-bearing timeline still refuses `CAPTION_RENDER_POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; that refusal does not block committing the pending MoveClips gesture.

## Decision

Officially admit invert of a prior committed reject-mode `MoveClips` of a video host by +1s with `includeLinks=false` that is not the undo tip. Only that command's ignore-links write set is restored when those fields still match the command's after-image. A later unrelated `SetMarkers`, a later text edit of the unmoved absolute stay cue, or a later text edit of the followed moving cue, therefore does not conflict: invert restores the moving host's start, leaves the unmoved audio partner at its original start, restores link policy `preserve`, restores the stored offset, restores sequence duration 3, restores the followed cue, and keeps the later markers, the unmoved absolute stay cue, and revised text. A later +0.5s includeLinks=false move of the same host, or invert of the tip, is whole-group `UNDO_CONFLICT` and does not roll the database back. Official workbench path extracts persistent audio so a linked partner exists, adds a followed hello cue before extract and an absolute stay cue after extract (via Make selected cue absolute), checks Ignore links and groups this move, multi-selects the video host, previews, and commits — not the single-clip Move button — and a later marker. Overwrite or insert paste invert, SetDissolve invert, and auto-close are not this slice's official success path. Captions and markers are not burned into preview or MP4; export stays a VTT sidecar.

## Consequences

I-005 and I-010 stay partial. The field-aware invert matrix now has an official persist path for includeLinks=false `MoveClips` of a video host after later markers or later cue text edits, alongside the already-delivered invert paths through insert-mode MoveClips. Overwrite or insert PasteClips invert, SetDissolve invert, invert of a non-last RippleDeleteClips / leave-gap DeleteClip / LiftClips, auto-close-on-move, new-track, cross-project bake, OS clipboard, source-interval crop, marker sidecar formats, MP4 burn-in, video/caption mute/solo, `syncLock`, the table's 0.02s off-grid offset, and I-006 remain out of this slice.
